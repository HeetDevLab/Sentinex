Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 24 July 2026

Sentinex ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app operates and how user data is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Sentinex does not collect, store, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or location.

All data used by the app (such as locked apps, intruder photos, and security logs) is stored locally on your device only and is not transmitted to any external servers.

2. Permissions We Use

Sentinex requires certain permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Biometric Permission: Used only to allow fingerprint authentication for unlocking protected apps.
  • Notification Permission: Used to display important security alerts and protection status.
  • Usage Access Permission: Used to detect which application is currently running in order to apply app lock protection.
  • Camera Permission: Used only to capture intruder photos when incorrect PIN attempts are made.
  • Media/Storage Permission: Used to save and access intruder photos locally on your device. No media is uploaded to our servers.

These permissions are used strictly for app functionality and not for collecting user data.

Permissions are requested only when required for the corresponding feature.

3. Data Storage

All data generated by Sentinex, including intruder photos and security logs, is stored securely on your device. We do not upload or back up this data to any cloud or remote servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties. Since no personal data is collected, no data is shared externally.

5. Security

We are committed to ensuring that your information remains secure. Since all data is stored locally, you have full control over your data.

6. Children’s Privacy

Sentinex is intended for general users. If you are under the age required in your region to use apps independently, please use the app with the supervision of a parent or guardian.

7. Advertising

Sentinex uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect limited device information as described in Google's own privacy policy. Sentinex itself does not collect or sell your personal data.

For more information about how Google processes data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:
