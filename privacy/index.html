Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 17 August 2026

Sentinex ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app operates and how user data is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Sentinex itself does not directly collect, store, or share personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or location.

Data used by the app, such as locked apps, intruder photos, and security logs, is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded by Sentinex to its own external servers.

2. Permissions We Use

Sentinex requires certain permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Biometric Permission: Used to allow fingerprint or other supported biometric authentication for unlocking protected apps.
  • Notification Permission: Used to display important security alerts and protection status.
  • Usage Access Permission: Used to detect which application is currently running in order to apply app-lock protection.
  • Camera Permission: Used to capture intruder photos when incorrect PIN attempts trigger the intruder protection feature.
  • Media/Storage Permission: Used to save and access intruder photos locally on your device. Sentinex does not upload these photos to its own servers.
  • Overlay Permission: Used to display the app-lock screen when a protected application is opened.
  • Foreground Service: Used to maintain app-lock protection while the protection service is active.
  • Boot Permission: Used to restart protection after the device is restarted.

These permissions are used for app functionality and are not used by Sentinex itself to collect personal information.

Permissions are requested only when required for the corresponding feature.

3. Data Storage

Data generated by Sentinex, including intruder photos and security logs, is stored locally on your device. Sentinex does not upload or back up this data to its own cloud or remote servers.

4. Data Sharing

Sentinex does not sell, trade, or share your personal app data with third parties.

Third-party services used by the app, such as Google AdMob, may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Security

We are committed to helping keep your information secure. App data generated by Sentinex is stored locally on your device, giving you control over that data.

6. Children's Privacy

Sentinex is intended for general users. If you are under the age required in your region to use apps independently, please use the app with the supervision of a parent or guardian.

7. Advertising

Sentinex uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, and usage data, to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, subject to Google's policies.

For more information about how Google processes data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
